## The Stock Market Has a Memory

For over 130 years, certain patterns have repeated themselves in financial markets. The January Effect, Sell in May, the Turn-of-Month — these are not myths, but statistically verifiable phenomena.

The problem: this data has until now been either hard to access, poorly presented, or analyzed uncritically. That is exactly what we are changing with **SeasonAlpha.ai**.

## What Is SeasonAlpha?

SeasonAlpha is an analysis platform that makes seasonal patterns visible for **stocks, ETFs, indices, futures, and crypto** — interactively, data-driven, and with the friendly support of AI. With AI we ask whether seasonal patterns are stable — and whether we can rely on them, or not.

No gut feelings. No guesses into the blue. Instead: what do 5, 10, 20, or 130 years of stock market history say about today? And what do our AI models say about it?

## What to Expect

### Seasonal Analysis on 7 Time Levels

From the decade down to the individual weekday — SeasonAlpha breaks the market down into its recurring components:

- **Decade cycle**: up to 131 years of price history for the Dow Jones (less for other markets, but still substantial), analyzed by decade
- **Annual cycle**: seasonal trajectory within the year, also known as the solar cycle. Supplemented by percentile bands, confidence intervals, and significance tests
- **Presidential cycle**: do not underestimate the power of four years of American presidency — expect fascinating analyses
- **Monthly cycle**: the trajectory within a month (Trading Day of Month — or TDOM for short)
- **Weekdays**: Monday anomaly, overnight vs. intraday split
- **Month-end**: the Turn-of-Month effect — one of the strongest seasonal edges
- **Moon phases**: full moon, new moon, supermoon — no esoteric nonsense. Let the data speak for itself.
- **January Trifecta**: the trajectory indicator par excellence. Three January indicators as a traffic light system for the whole year.
- **More to Come**: you can already look forward to new features that will be rolled out step by step.

### AI-Powered Analysis

SeasonAlpha uses machine learning not as a marketing buzzword, but as a genuine tool:

- **Pattern matching**: which historical year resembles 2026 the most?
- **Anomaly detection**: where is the market currently deviating from its seasonal pattern?
- **Significance tests**: is a pattern statistically robust or just coincidence?
- **Outlier management**: filter out crash years so they don't distort the analysis
